Four Mini-Games for Prime Video

Slumber Party Games  ·  2022–2023

Four browser mini-games, each built in four weeks, commissioned to promote Prime Video series — The Boys, Invincible, El Internado, and Fleabag. Each game had to hold at least 45 minutes of live-streamed content, which meant designing for reactability, not just completion. The campaign was featured by streamer Mayichi on Twitch.

  • Sole designer and programmer — core mechanics to final build
  • Client-facing throughout: needs assessment, regular test builds
  • Playtested all levels; built engine tools to speed up level authoring
  • Composed and implemented all music and sound
  • Assisted with extra art tasks across titles
