Blueprints

BeginnerGameplay
Who it's for
Artists and designers who have never coded, or coders who are new to Unreal.
What it's for
Making things happen in a level: doors, pickups, UI, simple enemies and game rules.
What you walk away with
A small playable prototype you built alone, with logic you can still read a month later.

Materials & HLSL

IntermediateTech Art
Who it's for
Artists who already use Unreal and want materials that look better and run faster.
What it's for
Every surface in the game: landscapes, props, VFX and stylised shading.
What you walk away with
A set of reusable master materials and the habit of checking what each one costs.

PCG

AdvancedEnvironments
Who it's for
Environment and tech artists who are tired of placing every rock by hand.
What it's for
Filling worlds with rules instead of clicks: forests, towns, scattering and paths.
What you walk away with
A PCG graph that dresses a level for you, with controls you can hand to a team.

Optimization

IntermediatePerformance
Who it's for
Anyone whose project runs fine on their own PC and nowhere else.
What it's for
Hitting a frame rate on real hardware with Nanite, Lumen, LODs and the profiler.
What you walk away with
You can profile a scene, find what is slow and fix it instead of guessing.

Procedural Modeling

AdvancedHoudini
Who it's for
3D and tech artists curious about Houdini. No previous Houdini needed.
What it's for
Generating assets, terrain and destruction from parameters instead of modeling each one.
What you walk away with
A procedural asset or terrain tool in Unreal that you can change in seconds.

Development in Unity

BeginnerUnity
Who it's for
Beginners and small indie or student teams working in Unity.
What it's for
C# gameplay scripting, scene structure and keeping a project from turning into chaos.
What you walk away with
A small finished Unity project with a structure your team can keep building on.

Pipeline tools

AdvancedPython
Who it's for
Tech artists and artists who repeat the same steps every day.
What it's for
Python and Houdini tools that automate exports, naming, setup and other boring parts.
What you walk away with
At least one tool your team actually uses, and the know-how to write the next one.

Game Design

BeginnerDesign
Who it's for
Students and developers with ideas who struggle to turn them into a game.
What it's for
Core loops, mechanics, systems thinking and the documents that keep a team aligned.
What you walk away with
A clear design document and a prototype that tests whether the idea is fun.

Level Design

BeginnerDesign
Who it's for
Beginners and designers who want levels that guide the player without signs everywhere.
What it's for
Spatial flow, pacing and blockout methodology, from greybox to playable.

A blocked-out, playtested level and a method you can repeat on the next one.

F.A.Q.

Online or in person? Which languages? I've never opened Unreal. Problem? Which version do you teach? Do I need a powerful PC? How much does it cost?
Answer

Online or in person?

Both. Private classes run remotely or in person in Barcelona and Sweden. Studio training can be on site or remote, and I give seminars at universities all around Europe.

Which languages?

Spanish, English and Catalan.

I've never opened Unreal. Problem?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. We start from the editor itself and build up from there. The depth adjusts to you; the approach doesn't.

Which version do you teach?

Unreal Engine 5.8, the current release. If your team is locked to an older version, we work in that one.

Do I need a powerful PC?

This is Epic's recommended setup for Unreal Engine 5.8. Below that, Unreal still opens, just slower. Send me your specs before the first class and we'll plan around them.

OS
Windows 11
CPU
Quad-core Intel or AMD, 2.5 GHz or faster
RAM
32 GB
GPU
DirectX 12 compatible, 8 GB VRAM or more
Lumen / Nanite
NVIDIA RTX 2000, AMD RX 6000, Intel Arc A-Series or newer

How much does it cost?

It depends on the format and the number of hours. Write to me and I'll give you a straight number. The first class is free either way.
